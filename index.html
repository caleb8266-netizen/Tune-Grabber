🎵 Tune Grabber

Paste a YouTube link, preview the video, and save the sound as an MP3.

FFmpeg isn't installed, so MP3 conversion won't work yet. See the README for the one-line install.

How do I get it into Apple Music?
  • On a Mac: keep “Add to Apple Music automatically” checked and it shows up in your library by itself. Or drag the downloaded MP3 into the Music app.
  • On Windows: open iTunes → File → Add File to Library… and pick the MP3.
  • Get it on your iPhone: turn on Sync Library (Settings → Music on iPhone, Music → Settings → General on Mac) and it syncs over, or plug your phone in and sync it from Finder/iTunes.

Use this for your own videos, royalty‑free music, or content you have permission to download.
