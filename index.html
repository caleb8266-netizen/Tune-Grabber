🔑 Using your YouTube sign-in for videos that need it ·
Use your YouTube sign-in?
Tune Grabber borrows the sign-in already saved in a web browser on the computer running it. Nothing is stored or shared. Be signed in to YouTube in that browser first.
On a Mac, a box may ask for your Mac password to let Python read the browser's saved data. Type it and click Always Allow.
Safari needs one extra step: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access → turn on Terminal, then restart Tune Grabber.

How do I get it into Apple Music?
  • Easiest (from iPhone or computer): leave Add to Apple Music on. The song goes into the Music library on your Mac, and with Sync Library turned on (Settings → Apps → Music on iPhone, Music → Settings → General on Mac) it shows up on your iPhone.
  • Saved to your iPhone: tap Save MP3. It goes to Files → Downloads, where you can play it. iPhones can't import MP3s into the Music app by themselves.
  • On Windows: open iTunes → File → Add File to Library… and pick the MP3, then sync your iPhone.
